# 公告界面素材

公告底图.png 使用内置 image_gen 生成；上下页按钮复用主包黄色按钮，关闭图标使用单独生成的公告关闭按钮.png。标题、正文、页码均为可编辑的 Cocos 节点，不烘焙到图片。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: production game announcement panel background sprite, not a screenshot. Make a single landscape 3:2 front-facing symmetrical popup frame for a colorful Chinese 2D chibi military extraction shooter. Thick comic ink outlines, hand-painted bevels, dark navy gunmetal, bright yellow caution-stripe accents, small restrained cyan indicator lights, matching an industrial military supply-shop UI. Large blank yellow title plaque along top center, very spacious flat dark desaturated navy central writing area with no decorations in its middle, lower dark empty footer for pagination. Thin armored corner plates and screws, tidy polished readable silhouette. Absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, close buttons, arrows, characters, weapons, inventory slots, watermark or background scene. No perspective tilt. Entire frame visible with tight transparent margins. Genuine transparent alpha outside the panel, opaque inside. Title zone top 14%, writing zone middle 68%, footer bottom 15%. One continuous uncluttered panel, no split columns.

## 编辑公告

修改 DLC/Scripts/Panel/ZRSJZ_NoticePanel.ts 中的 Notices 数组。每一项含 Title、Version、Content，最新项放最前，旧项保留。正文支持换行和滚动；首尾翻页按钮自动禁用。

按设备本地日期记录手动关闭时间，次日再次进入主页恢复自动弹出。空白遮罩仅挡点击，右上角关闭按钮才会记为当天已关闭。

## 公告专用关闭按钮

使用内置 image_gen 单独生成，绑定公告预制体的关闭节点；其他界面不变。

Front-facing orthographic, no tilt, centered, occupies 92% of canvas. Two tiny cyan light accents at sides and subtle metallic edge wear matching a dark navy and yellow caution-striped announcement panel in a chibi Chinese extraction shooter.
